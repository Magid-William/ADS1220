# Perfboard layout - ADS1220 front-end (16 x 8 holes)

Board: 16 columns x 8 rows at 2.54 mm pitch = **38.1 x 17.78 mm** (single-sided dotted perfboard, no strips).

Generated by `gen.py` - do not hand-edit; change the data in the script and re-run.

Rules the layout obeys:

- every hole is soldered to at most one net;
- no wire runs over a hole of a different net;
- no component body sits on top of another net's hole;
- the ADS1220 module is **socketed** (2x8 female header): two wires pass underneath it.

## 4. Build order

1. Solder the 2x8 female socket in columns 2 and 8, rows A-H (DRDY at A2). Keep the socket straight - the module plugs in from above.
2. Solder the four 1.2k resistors (R2a, R2b, R1a, R1b).
3. Solder C1, C2, C3.
4. Wire the digital side: the four SPI/DRDY jumpers and the two ground/3V3 runs on column 1.
5. Wire the analog side: REFP0->R2a, the two ground tap wires, the 3V3 run to AVDD, then the under-module ground wire (step 6).
6. The one wire that passes underneath the module: E8 -> E7 -> F7 -> G7 -> H7 -> H8. Route it flat against the board before plugging the module in.
7. Plug in the module and land the off-board wires (section 2).
8. Print `board.pdf` at 100 % and tape it to the board as a soldering guide.

## 5. Before you power it up

- continuity: each net in section 2 should beep only within itself;
- no continuity between `3V3` and `GND`, or between the analog pins (AIN0/1/2) and anything else;
- `AIN3` (D8) must be floating;
- with the driver polling: V(a)-V(b) >= 0.75 V, V(x) ~ V(y) ~ V(a)/2.

## 6. Under-module routing

These holes are inside the module footprint and carry wires: E7, F7, G7, H7. They only work with the socket fitted - do not solder the module directly to the board.
